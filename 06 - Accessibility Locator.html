01 · The Problem

THE CITY WAS
NOT BUILT FOR THEM.

Urban environments are designed for the able-bodied and the young. For elderly individuals with mobility limitations, every outing is a challenge — not because they lack the will, but because the infrastructure fails them.

🪜

Stairs Everywhere

Most public buildings, markets, and transport stops lack ramp access — making them unreachable for those with walkers or wheelchairs.

🪑

No Rest Points

Long stretches without seating force elderly individuals to cut trips short or risk exhaustion and falls in public spaces.

🛗

Missing Elevators

Multi-floor public buildings without lifts are effectively inaccessible. Many elderly avoid these spaces entirely.

😰

Fear of Going Out

The uncertainty of whether a destination is accessible causes anxiety — many elderly simply stop venturing outside.

🗺️

No Accessibility Data

There is no centralised, elder-focused resource to check whether a location has ramps, lifts, or seating before travelling.

🏚️

Social Withdrawal

When mobility barriers go unaddressed, elderly individuals stop participating in community life — deepening isolation.

05 · Smart Features

INTELLIGENT.
INCLUSIVE.

🗺️

Map-Based Display

All accessible places shown on an embedded map. Distance-sorted. No complex navigation required — just tap and go.

⚙️

Multi-Filter Search

Filter by ramp, lift, seating, accessible toilet, and disabled parking simultaneously. Results update instantly.

🧭

Open in Google Maps

Every result includes a one-tap "Open in Maps" button — no copying addresses, no confusion. Just go.

🤝

Community Contribution

Users and caregivers can report new accessible places or flag outdated information — improving the database over time.

📴

Offline Access

Cached location data means the locator works even without an active internet connection — critical in low-coverage areas.

🎙️

Voice Search

"Find me a hospital with a lift" — voice commands make the search fully hands-free for users with limited dexterity.
