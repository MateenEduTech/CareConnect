01 · The Problem

Forgotten pills.
Preventable harm.

Medication non-adherence is one of the most avoidable yet devastating causes of health deterioration in elderly populations. The consequences are serious — and entirely preventable.

01

Memory Decline

Natural cognitive aging makes it genuinely difficult to remember multiple medicines across multiple daily time windows.

02

Complex Schedules

Many elderly patients manage 4–8 medications with different timings, food conditions, and dosages — impossible to track mentally.

03

Dosage Confusion

Without clear tracking, double-dosing and under-dosing both occur — each with serious health consequences.

04

Caregiver Blindness

Families and caregivers have no visibility into whether medicines were taken. They only learn of problems after the harm is done.

05

No Tracking Tools

Existing reminder apps are designed for tech-savvy users. Elderly individuals need something far simpler and more supportive.

06

Hospitalisation Risk

Studies show missed medications account for nearly 10% of elderly hospitalisations — a health system cost that is entirely avoidable.

Designed for the
generation that needs it most.

Every detail of the interface is optimised for elderly users — from touch target sizes to colour choices to the words we use.

  • 🔔
    Smart Reminders

    Sound, visual, and vibration alerts — repeated gently if not acknowledged within 5 minutes.

  • ⚠️
    Missed Dose Escalation

    Two misses in a row → caregiver notified. Three in a row → AI suggests contacting a doctor.

  • 📊
    Weekly Adherence Report

    Visual summary of the week — which medicines were taken, missed, or skipped — shared with family.

  • 🎙️
    Voice Command Support

    "I took my medicine" — voice confirmation so no tapping is needed for those with limited mobility.

  • 🔗
    Help Nearby Trigger

    If medicine is missed AND elder hasn't responded, a Help Nearby request is auto-suggested.
