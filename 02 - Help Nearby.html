01 · The Problem

Small needs.
Big loneliness.

Millions of elderly individuals face daily challenges that seem minor — but without timely help, they quietly erode independence, dignity, and joy.

🚶

Mobility Barriers

Simple errands like collecting medicine or groceries become impossible obstacles for those with limited mobility.

🔇

Hesitation to Ask

Pride, guilt, and fear of being a burden prevent elderly individuals from reaching out even when they desperately need help.

🕰️

No Quick Access

Existing systems are built for emergencies. There is no infrastructure for the small, everyday help that matters most.

🏚️

Isolated Networks

With family scattered across cities, the local community remains untapped — a ready resource with no activation mechanism.

💬

Communication Gap

Technology exists but is too complex. Elderly users are left behind by interfaces designed for younger, tech-savvy audiences.

😔

Silent Loneliness

When needs go unmet, it isn't just physical suffering — it's the slow emotional toll of feeling invisible and forgotten.

Three roles.
One network of care.

👴
The Elder

Designed for simplicity.
Built for speed.

Every pixel of the interface is optimised for elderly users. Large buttons, high contrast, voice command support, and a two-step flow ensure that no one is left behind due to technology.

  • One oversized "I Need Help" button — impossible to miss
  • Category icons with labels — no guesswork required
  • Nearby helpers visible instantly — builds confidence
  • Voice command fallback — "Hey, I need help with medicine"
  • Font size minimum 18px — readable at a glance
  • High-contrast dark and light modes available
  • Auto-escalation if no response within 5 minutes
05 · Intelligence Layer

Smart by design.
Human at heart.

Behind the simple interface lies a thoughtful system that ensures the right person reaches the right elder at the right time.

📍

Location-Based Matching

The system identifies helpers within a configurable radius. Closest available volunteer gets first priority — minimising wait time.

⏱️

Time-Based Escalation

If no volunteer accepts within 3 minutes, the request expands its radius and alerts the next layer. Family is notified at 5 minutes.

🔶

Request Categorisation

Requests are tagged as Urgent or Normal. Fall-triggered requests receive highest priority and bypass queue entirely.

✅

Verified Helper Profiles

Community members register with basic verification. Ratings and completion history build trust over time — creating accountability.

👁️

Live Request Tracking

Families and caregivers receive real-time updates — request sent, accepted, completed. Full transparency. No anxious waiting.

🔔

Passive Fall Trigger

When the Fall Detection module activates, Help Nearby is automatically triggered — no manual action required from the elder.
